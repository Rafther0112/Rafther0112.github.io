Research

How much of what a cell does is written in its fluctuations

My undergraduate physics thesis argued that the variance between genetically identical cells carries functional regulatory information rather than noise to be averaged away. Modern single-cell machine learning keeps rediscovering the same quantity from the data side, through within-cell-type correlation structure. The two literatures barely cite each other.

Building the generative machinery that can actually read that information is the work I want to do for the next five years.

Generative models and flow matching

Equivariant graph neural networks, flow matching over structured scientific data, and simulation-based inference for mechanistic models.

Single-cell multi-omics

scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq and multiome data, enhancer to gene regulatory links, and the methods that make them learnable.

Biophysics and information theory

Stochastic dynamical systems, stochastic gene expression, and information-cost trade-offs in regulatory network motifs.

Biomedical vision

Topology-aware segmentation of tubular structures, with losses and metrics that score connectivity instead of volume overlap.

Machine learning for matter

Crystal structure prediction, interatomic potentials, and benchmarks that measure what a DFT practitioner actually cares about.

A bipartite surface metric and a surface distance loss with explicit removal and creation terms, plus instance weighting by inverse squared radius so that thin distal branches count. Evaluated on nnU-Net, SwinUNETR and NexToU over ImageCAS and ASOCA.
